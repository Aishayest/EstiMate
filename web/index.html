How big is this task? A guess, and an honest range.

Software teams size their tasks in story points: a rough unit of effort, where 1 is tiny and 8 is big. Paste a task and EstiMate suggests its size, plus a range it has proven it can be trusted with.

Where

A software team

We ran EstiMate on our own team's tasks during my internship. Those tasks are internal, so they are not shown on this site.

How

In sprint planning

The team lead looked at the suggested size and range, and brought the system in step by step to get a rough idea of how long a task would take.

What changed

Better sizing, smaller tasks

The team adopted it. Sizing tasks and judging how long they would take got better, it helped split work into smaller tasks, and tasks were discussed in more detail.

All numbers elsewhere on this site come from the public Deep-SE data, not from the team's tasks.
